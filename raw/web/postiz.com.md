## Frequently asked questions

**Can Postiz auto-repeat or recycle evergreen posts?**
Yes. Any post can be set to repeat on a schedule, so your best-performing content keeps going out without you copy-pasting it every month. Pick the post, set the cadence (for example, every 30 days), choose which channels it should hit each time, and Postiz handles the rest. Each repost shows up in your calendar so you can still see, edit, or pause upcoming runs. On Team, Pro, and Ultimate plans you can also auto-post from an RSS feed, which is handy for turning a blog or newsletter into a steady social cadence.

**Does Postiz have a public API and webhooks?**
Yes. Every paid plan includes a REST API for creating posts, uploading media, and managing integrations — useful if you want to plug Postiz into your own tools, n8n, Zapier, or a custom dashboard. Webhooks are included from Standard and up, with the monthly allowance scaling by plan: Standard 2, Team 10, Pro 30, Ultimate unlimited. Combined with the public API, they let you trigger posts automatically from CI, a CMS, or any event source.

**What analytics does Postiz provide?**
Postiz shows per-channel and per-post analytics pulled from each network's official insights API. Depending on the channel, you'll see impressions, likes, comments, shares, reach, and engagement rate. Everything is in one dashboard — you can compare how the same post performed on LinkedIn vs. X vs. Instagram without opening five different apps. Analytics coverage depends on what each platform exposes publicly, so some smaller networks are more limited than the major ones.

**Can I cross-post the same content to multiple platforms at once?**
Yes — that's the core of Postiz. Write a post once, pick the channels, and Postiz publishes to all of them at the time you choose. Before scheduling, each channel gets its own preview so you can tweak copy, media, or hashtags per platform — for example, a longer LinkedIn version and a shorter X version of the same post. You can also queue the same post to re-run on an evergreen schedule.

**What AI features does Postiz include?**
Postiz ships with a built-in AI agent that can draft posts, generate images, and produce short videos — all from one chat window. AI text — hooks, captions, threads, and hashtags tuned per platform. Included on every paid plan. AI images — generate visuals directly from a prompt and attach them to a post. Monthly allowance: Standard 20, Team 100, Pro 300, Ultimate 500. AI video — short AI-generated clips. Monthly allowance: Standard 3, Team 10, Pro 30, Ultimate 60. You can also chat with the agent to schedule a post end-to-end: "write me a LinkedIn post about X, make a matching image, schedule it for Tuesday at 9am."

**Can I manage multiple brands or clients from a single Postiz account?**
Yes. On Team, Pro, and Ultimate plans you can organize your connected accounts into customer groups, so each brand or client has its own clean workspace inside one Postiz login. Each customer group keeps its own channels, calendar, and analytics — useful for agencies, freelancers, or anyone running several brands. You pick the plan based on the total number of social channels you need to connect across every brand.

**How many team members can I invite, and what can they do?**
Team, Pro, and Ultimate plans let you invite unlimited team members by email. Standard is a solo plan. Postiz has two roles: Admin — manage channels, billing, and team settings. Member — draft, schedule, and review posts. Everyone works from the same calendar, so drafts, scheduled posts, and analytics stay in one place.

**How much does Postiz cost, and what's included in each plan?**
Postiz has four paid plans. All prices are monthly; annual billing saves about 20%. Standard — $29/mo. 5 channels, 400 posts/month, AI text, 20 AI images, 3 AI videos, public API, 2 webhooks. Team — $39/mo. 10 channels, unlimited posts, team members, 100 AI images, 10 AI videos, auto-posting from RSS, 10 webhooks. Pro — $49/mo. 30 channels, unlimited posts, team members, 300 AI images, 30 AI videos, 30 webhooks. Ultimate — $99/mo. 100 channels, unlimited posts, team members, 500 AI images, 60 AI videos. Most solo creators pick Standard. Small agencies managing a handful of brands usually go Team or Pro. Ultimate is for agencies and large teams connecting many accounts.

**Can I swap or change my connected channels after choosing a plan?**
Yes. You're not locked into any specific set of channels. Connect, disconnect, and swap any of the 30+ supported networks at any time, as long as your total active channels stay within your plan's limit. If you downgrade and exceed the new limit, Postiz will automatically disable the most recent connections. Reconnect the ones you want to keep and disable the others — nothing is deleted.

**If I connect 3 Facebook pages, does that count as 1 channel or 3?**
Each connected social account is one channel. So three Facebook pages = three channels, not one. Channel allowance by plan: Standard — 5 channels, Team — 10 channels, Pro — 30 channels, Ultimate — 100 channels. If you manage multiple brands and each has its own Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn, count one slot per account you plan to connect.

## Architecture (How it works) — https://docs.postiz.com/howitworks
Postiz operates through a three-service architecture within a single Docker container, complemented by four external services.
- Frontend Service — provides the web interface, relying on the backend for "Schedule posts, Show analytics, Manage users."
- Backend Service — functions as the system's coordinator, triggering Temporal workflows for asynchronous operations including social media posting and token refresh.
- Orchestrator Service — manages Temporal workflows and activities, handling "Posting scheduled content to social media platforms, Refreshing tokens from different social media platforms, Sending digest and notification emails."
- External dependencies: Temporal (durable workflow engine), Redis (session management and caching), SQL Database (typically Postgres), Storage (local file system).
- Temporal provides "Reliable scheduling," "Task queues," "Workflow visibility," and "Durable state" to ensure reliable async operations and prevent data loss during service restarts.

## MCP Introduction — https://docs.postiz.com/mcp/introduction
The Model Context Protocol (MCP) enables AI agents to interact with Postiz through a standardized tool interface. Users can connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or other MCP-compatible clients to manage social media accounts using natural language commands.

The Postiz MCP server provides nine tools: integrationList (view connected social accounts with optional filtering), groupList (access organization groups/customers), integrationSchema (retrieve platform-specific posting requirements), triggerTool (execute platform helpers, e.g. channel listings), schedulePostTool (create, schedule, or publish posts immediately), generateImageTool (produce AI-generated images), generateVideoOptions (display video generation capabilities), videoFunctionTool (access video generator configurations), generateVideoTool (generate video content).

Authentication: Bearer Token — connect via `https://api.postiz.com/mcp` with your API key as a Bearer token (works for both API keys and OAuth tokens prefixed with `pos_`); or URL-Embedded Key — use `https://api.postiz.com/mcp/your-api-key` format.

Agents automatically discover available tools, understand their parameters, and execute actions on your behalf. Limitation: the current MCP implementation cannot read or reply to comments; it's restricted to reading integration data and writing posts/media content.

## Public API Overview — https://docs.postiz.com/public-api/introduction
The Postiz API enables developers to programmatically schedule and publish content across 32 social media platforms. Authentication uses API keys or OAuth2 tokens, with a base URL of `https://api.postiz.com/public/v1` for cloud deployments.
- API Key: retrieved from Settings > Developers > Public API.
- OAuth2: for multi-user applications, tokens begin with `pos_`.
- Rate limits: 90 requests/hour on the create-post endpoint for self-hosted instances; cloud services allow 100 requests/hour.
- Supported platforms: 32 platforms across social media (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok), community spaces (Reddit, Discord, Slack), blogging services (Medium, Dev.to, WordPress), and business tools (Google My Business). Platforms without custom requirements need only a `__type` identifier.
- Post payload: scheduling date and type (`schedule` or `now`), integration IDs linking to connected accounts, content, images, and platform-specific settings, tags and short link preferences.
- Image handling: two-step process — upload via `/upload` endpoint receiving an image ID, then reference that ID in post creation.
- Official SDKs exist for NodeJS and n8n integrations, with provider-specific documentation for each platform's settings schema.

## CLI Introduction — https://docs.postiz.com/cli/introduction
The Postiz CLI automates social media posting across 28+ platforms through a command-line interface, wrapping the Public API for scheduling, integration management, and media uploads. Installation via npm or pnpm with global install flags.
Authentication options: OAuth2 device flow (recommended, no credentials required, uses `postiz auth:login`); API key method (set `POSTIZ_API_KEY` environment variable); self-hosted instances (configure with `POSTIZ_API_URL`).
Key commands: integration management (`integrations:list`, `integrations:settings`), post operations (`posts:create`, `posts:list`, `posts:delete`), analytics retrieval (`analytics:platform`, `analytics:post`), media uploading (`upload`).
"All commands output JSON, making the CLI easy to use in scripts and automation pipelines."

## Providers Overview — https://docs.postiz.com/providers/overview
> Configure social media providers for Postiz

You can see all the providers that Postiz supports documented in the sidebar, under "**Providers**".

Please note that no providers are configured by default. You will need to configure them all in your `.env` file, or as environment variables. You will need to restart Postiz whenever you change environment variables. If you are using docker compose, you must run `docker compose down` and then `docker compose up` to rebuild the containers with the updated variables.

### Available Providers (in-app connect UI)
X (Twitter), LinkedIn, LinkedIn Page, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, YouTube, Google My Business, TikTok, Reddit, Pinterest, Discord, Slack, Telegram, Dribbble, Skool, Whop, MeWe, Farcaster.

### Providers available via Public API only
These platforms are fully supported for scheduling and publishing through the Public API but don't currently have an in-app channel-connect UI page. Configure their env-var keys (see Configuration Reference) and use them via the API: Twitch, Kick, VK, Nostr, Lemmy, Medium, Dev.to, Hashnode, WordPress, Listmonk.
