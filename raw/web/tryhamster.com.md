## Landing page — https://tryhamster.com/

AI-native engineers ship code 10x faster in isolation, but the bottleneck for teams is refining what to build, aligning on design, and iterating prototypes. Hamster keeps your team in sync, removes busy-work, and ensures AI delivers to plan; so iterations sail through review, and ship fast.

Used by teams at some of our favourite companies: NVIDIA, Google, Vercel, Notion, Canva, Shopify, Amazon, Adobe, DoorDash, Pinterest, Nubank, Sentry.

## Refine ideas and align with your team in meetings minutes.

We're all builders now, with complementary skills and perspectives. Hamster's multi-player AI helps you refine and align on what to build up front, so you stay in control of delivery, and AI does the work.

## Sprint planning, grooming, story points — gone.

Those ceremonies existed to keep your team in sync on what you're building and who's doing it. Now Hamster does that for you.

## You decide what to build

Every tool you have today was built for humans doing the work. Hamster is for teams where agents deliver. You set the direction — the reasoning, constraints, and customer context — and Hamster captures it in a brief your whole team can align on.

Agents without direction deliver the wrong thing fast. Briefs make sure they deliver the right thing.

Integrations: Github, Drive, Notion, Linear, Jira

## AI handles how it gets built

Hamster generates a structured plan from your brief — scoped tasks with acceptance criteria that any coding agent can execute. The agent gets the full context: the brief, the reasoning, and every decision your team made.

## Works with any tool

Pull your plan into any AI coding tool. Hamster gives every agent the context it needs to build exactly what you want.

AI coding tools supported: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Grok, Taskmaster, Cursor, Copilot, Roo Code, Windsurf, Aider, Cline, Amp.

## Your backlog doesn't need to be groomed. It needs a brief.

Connect your issue tracker, and Hamster reads your tickets, writes the brief, and fills in the reasoning, context, and decisions that were never captured.

Two-way sync keeps your tracker current. Tickets close as tasks complete. Your team and leadership stay informed in the tools they already use.

## Your agent already knows what to build

The Hamster CLI syncs your briefs, tasks, and plans into your repo. Your coding agent reads them directly — the full brief, the reasoning, the team's decisions. Not just a task title.

## Getting Started — https://tryhamster.com/docs/hamster-studio/getting-started

Sign up at tryhamster.com. Recommended path:

1. Get familiar with the concepts — Goals, Initiatives, Briefs, Blueprints, Methods, Context Graph.
2. Populate your Context Graph — Connect GitHub, Slack, Linear/Jira, Figma, Notion, Google Drive, Meeting Agent.
3. Draft your first Blueprints — English descriptions of stable things in your business (products, systems, teams). Hamster reads the Context Graph and drafts Blueprints on request.
4. Take an idea from hunch to delivery:
   - Chat about the idea → Hamster turns it into a brief grounded in Blueprints and context.
   - Refine in chat → Generate a plan → The Brief becomes a structured Task breakdown.
   - Hand off to a Cloud Agent, which reads the Plan and opens a PR.
   - Once familiar, invite teammates: Reviewer seats are free and unlimited.

## Briefs — https://tryhamster.com/docs/hamster-studio/briefs

A Brief is the spine of Hamster Studio — the single artifact that carries an idea from "we should do this" to a pull request. One Brief is one team-aligned unit of "what we're going to ship": title, description, Plan of parent Tasks and subtasks, tags, status, alignment votes, and a deliver button that hands work to Cloud Agents or the CLI.

Briefs are where the Refine → Align → Deliver loop plays out. Refine the Brief in chat until scope is sharp. Align the team by sharing it and collecting votes. Deliver by pressing one button and getting a PR back.

Subsections: Creating Briefs, Editing Briefs, Brief Status (Draft → Refining → Aligned → Shipping → Done), Brief Tags, Context Documents, Activity Timeline, Sharing Briefs, Delivering Briefs.

## Plans — https://tryhamster.com/docs/hamster-studio/plans

Plans are the output of asking Hamster Studio's AI to turn a Brief into deliverable work: an ordered list of parent Tasks, each broken down into subtasks with descriptions and acceptance criteria. Plans live inside the Brief they were generated from.

Subsections: Generating Plans, Guided Research (research flow to enrich Briefs before generating a Plan), Understanding Plans (task hierarchy, view options, deliver), Cloud Agents.

## Studio product — https://tryhamster.com/product/studio

Hamster Studio: "Brief it. Plan it. Ship it." An AI-native workspace for product teams. One place to think, align, plan, and deliver software.

Key features:
- **Briefs**: The AI-native product document. Write what you're building; AI fills in the gaps — expanding ideas, surfacing questions, keeping the team in sync. Every brief becomes the source of truth for engineers and AI agents.
- **Collaboration**: Multiple people edit the same brief at once with live cursors. @mention teammates. Every change versioned and restorable. Fork conversations into sub-threads.
- **Plans**: Click once — Hamster reads the brief, checks codebase and team context, generates a structured task breakdown with acceptance criteria. Tasks appear as they're created.
- **Agent Context**: The Hamster CLI syncs briefs, tasks, and plans into a `.hamster/` directory in the repo as markdown files. Agents read them directly — current context, always.
- **Hamster Horde (Coming Soon)**: Assign a task; AI opens the PR.
- **Connections**: Connect GitHub, Linear, Slack, Notion, Google Drive, Jira, and 35+ tools. AI searches across every connected source when writing or planning.
- **Blueprints**: Document vision, strategy, personas, and ways of working once. Hamster draws on blueprints for all planning and writing.

## Taskmaster product — https://tryhamster.com/product/taskmaster

Taskmaster: "Structure for AI. Sanity for you." AI agents are fast but directionless. Taskmaster gives them a structured task list with dependencies, context, and acceptance criteria.

Install: `curl -sSL https://tryhamster.com/cli/install | bash`

Workflow: PRD → Tasks → Subtasks → Done

Key features:
- **Complexity Analysis**: `tm analyze-complexity` scores each task 1–10 and recommends which to expand.
- **Research**: `tm research` queries live sources for latest best practices, security patches, and library updates (beyond knowledge cutoffs).
- **Autopilot / TDD**: State machine running Red → Green → Refactor cycle. Creates branches, runs tests, retries on failure.
- **Tagged Task Lists**: Isolated contexts for branches — use tags to keep task lists separate per feature.
- **MCP Support**: Native MCP server for Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, Claude Code, any terminal.
- **Open Source**: MIT licensed. Core always free.
- **Local Mode**: Fully offline with own API keys (Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Ollama, etc.). Works with Claude Code and Gemini CLI without a key.

Relationship to Studio: Taskmaster (solo developer + AI agent) → Hamster Studio (team alignment + AI execution). The CLI connects both. Already using Taskmaster? Studio is where your team aligns before agents execute.

The AI-native SDLC replaces traditional waterfall/agile with a human-AI loop: Brief → Align → Plan → Execute → Ship. Hamster and Taskmaster together implement this lifecycle. Planning in Hamster, execution with Taskmaster.
