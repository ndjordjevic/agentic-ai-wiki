## Questions, answered.

### What is Sauna?
Sauna is an AI agent that learns how you and your team work, remembers everything that matters, and acts on your behalf. The more context it has — your emails, documents, Slack messages, files — the smarter it gets and the more it can do.

### What tools and apps does Sauna connect to?
Sauna connects to over 3,000 tools and apps — Linear, GitHub, Notion, Gmail, Google Calendar, Jira, HubSpot, Stripe, and more. You can also add custom MCP connectors for tools not in the connections list.

### Does Sauna keep working when I go offline?
Yes. Sauna runs in the cloud around the clock — drafting messages in your voice, tracking what matters, filing tickets, delivering briefings. You don't have to be online for work to move forward.

### Where can I use Sauna?
Wherever you work: the web app, iOS, iMessage, Slack, or email. One agent, the same memory, on every surface.

### Can my whole team use Sauna together?
Yes — Sauna is multiplayer. Teammates share a Sauna's context and memory, so it can coordinate work across the team instead of starting from scratch with each person.

### Is my data secure?
Yes. Sauna is SOC 2 compliant, and your data is never used to train models or shared with third parties. Your context is yours — private, encrypted, and only ever used to do work on your behalf.

### Memory — https://sauna.ai/learn/memory
Two memories that work together — workspace memory for what should stay true, session memory for what was said.

**Workspace memory** — durable notes under `memory/` and selected Markdown under `documents/` (top-level docs and project `ABOUT.md`). Not a transcript; refined long-term model. Loaded early in context.

**Session memory** — past conversations, other threads, older turns. Automatic session reminders (recent-thread summaries, moment-linked fragments) are skims, not transcripts. Deeper recall via archive search when stakes rise. Live chat wins over stale snippets.

### Multiplayer — https://sauna.ai/learn/multiplayer
Three ways to work with other people:
- **Spaces** — shared project workspace with its own knowledge, skills, schedules, and connections
- **Brain Access** — one person gets access to your whole Sauna (Read-only Brain or Full Brain)
- **Shared Folders** — access to a specific slice of knowledge (Read-only or Read + write)

All three require Sauna accounts on every side.
