## FAQs

### What is a website AI agent?

It's an AI-powered assistant that lives on your website and interacts with visitors through natural conversations. Unlike traditional chatbots that follow scripted flows, a website AI agent understands questions, answers using your content, guides visitors toward the right information or products, and helps achieve business outcomes such as lead generation, customer support, or online sales.

### How is CrafterQ different from a traditional website chatbot?

Traditional chatbots typically rely on predefined questions, decision trees, or limited knowledge. CrafterQ uses advanced AI to understand natural language, retrieve information from your website, documentation, FAQs, and files, and deliver accurate, conversational responses. It can also guide visitors toward actions like booking a demo, requesting a quote, or completing a purchase.

### What content can I train my AI agent on?

CrafterQ can learn from your website, documentation, knowledge base, FAQs, PDFs, Word documents, text files, and custom question-and-answer pairs. Your agent continuously uses this content to provide accurate answers and personalized guidance for your visitors.

### Does CrafterQ use ChatGPT or train public AI models on my data?

No. Your content remains under your control and is not used to train public AI models. CrafterQ uses your website and knowledge sources to generate answers for your visitors while keeping your business data private and secure.

### How long does it take to deploy a website AI agent?

Most businesses can create and deploy their first AI agent in just a few minutes. Simply add your website or upload your content, let CrafterQ build its knowledge, customize your agent, and embed it on your website with a single line of code.
