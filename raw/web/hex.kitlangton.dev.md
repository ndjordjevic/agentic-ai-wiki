# Hex

> Hex is local voice dictation for Mac, Windows, and Linux. Hold a shortcut, speak, and Hex transcribes on the device and pastes the text wherever the cursor is.

- Mac (Apple silicon, macOS 15 or later): https://downloads.hex.kitlangton.com/releases/HEX-latest-arm64.dmg, or `brew install --cask anomalyco/tap/hex`
- Windows 10 or 11 beta, x64 and ARM64: per-user Setup.exe installers at https://hex.kitlangton.com/
- Linux beta, x86_64 Arch with glibc 2.44 or later: `curl -fsSL https://downloads.hex.kitlangton.com/install-linux.sh | sh`
