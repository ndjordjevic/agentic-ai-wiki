Features

### Build, scale, govern and optimize enterprise grade AI agents

[Agent Platform](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/agents/overview) is our open and comprehensive platform that empowers businesses to rapidly build, scale, govern and optimize enterprise-grade agents grounded in your enterprise data. It provides the full-stack foundation and extensive developer choice you need to transform your applications and workflows into powerful agentic systems at global scale.

[Scale agents from Agent Platform to your organization. Use Gemini Enterprise app to securely register, manage, and govern your custom-built agents. Learn about Gemini Enterprise app](https://cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise/agents)

### Agent-powered development and workflows

Now available through Agent Platform, Google Antigravity provides a centralized app to steer, customize, and orchestrate agents. You can deploy multiple agents to simultaneously execute entire workflows like product launches—automating the code generation for your website, on-brand asset creation, and customer email production. [Download](https://antigravity.google/docs/enterprises) Antigravity and log in to the [desktop application](https://antigravity.google/docs/overview) or [Antigravity CLI](https://antigravity.google/docs/cli-overview) using your standard Google Cloud credentials.

### 200+ Google and third-party AI models and tools

Choose from Google's latest multimodal models like Gemini 3.5, third-party models like Anthropic's Claude Model Family, and open models like [Gemma](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/open-models/use-gemma) in [Model Garden](https://console.cloud.google.com/agent-platform/model-garden). You can also customize models to your use case with a variety of [tuning options](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models#model_customization_tuning).

Our [Model Evaluation service](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/evaluation-overview) provides enterprise-grade tools for objective, data-driven assessment of generative AI models.

[Quickstart: Get started with Gemini 3](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/start/get-started-with-gemini-3)

### Open and integrated AI platform

Data scientists can move faster with Agent Platform tools for training, tuning, and deploying ML models.

Agent platform [notebooks](https://cloud.google.com/products/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/notebooks), including your choice of Colab Enterprise or Workbench, are natively integrated with [BigQuery](https://cloud.google.com/bigquery) providing a single surface across all data and AI workloads.

Agent platform [Training](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/training/overview) and [Prediction](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/predictions/overview) help you reduce training time and deploy models to production easily with your choice of open source frameworks and optimized [AI infrastructure](https://cloud.google.com/ai-infrastructure).

### MLOps for predictive and generative AI

Agent Platform provides purpose-built [MLOps tools](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/start/introduction-mlops) for data scientists and ML engineers to automate, standardize, and manage ML projects.

Modular tools help you collaborate across teams and improve models throughout the entire development lifecycle—identify the best model for a use case with [Model Evaluation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/evaluation/introduction), orchestrate workflows with [Pipelines](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/pipelines/introduction), manage any model with [Model Registry](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/model-registry/introduction), serve/share/reuse ML features with [Feature Store](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/featurestore/latest/overview), and [monitor](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/featurestore/latest/monitor-features) models for input skew and drift.

How It Works

### Agent Platform provides several options for agent building, model training and deployment:

*   [Agent Platform](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform) enables you to build, scale, govern and optimize enterprise ready agents in one unified platform
*   [Agent Studio](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/agent-studio) gives you access to large generative AI models, including [Gemini 3](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/models/gemini/3-pro), so you can evaluate, tune, and deploy them for use in your AI-powered applications
*   [Model Garden](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models) lets you discover, test, customize, and deploy in Agent Platform and select open-source (OSS) models and assets
*   [Custom training](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning) gives you complete control over the training process, including using your preferred ML framework, writing your own training code, and choosing hyperparameter tuning options
