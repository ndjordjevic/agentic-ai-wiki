### What languages does ParakeetAI support?

ParakeetAI supports 50+ languages — you'll find the full list in the language dropdown when you start a session. It listens for one language at a time, so if you switch languages mid-call, update the session language to match.

### Does ParakeetAI support coding calls?

Yes. ParakeetAI offers full coding support, including code suggestions and explanations, and it works in both interviews and regular calls.

### Can I give ParakeetAI instructions to follow during the call?

ParakeetAI uses your uploaded CV or resume, any documents you've added, and anything you type into the Instructions field when you create a session. You can paste a lot in there — an entire job description or document is fine. You can also send ParakeetAI messages during the call itself.

### Can I use ParakeetAI for a phone call?

Yes. ParakeetAI supports phone calls over Google Voice. Start a session in the browser and click the phone icon for setup instructions.

### Does ParakeetAI have a mobile app?

A native ParakeetAI mobile app is on its way to the App Store and Google Play, but it isn't available to download just yet. In the meantime, use the mobile-optimized version directly in your browser: no download needed. Visit parakeet-ai.com on your phone and sign in.

### What operating systems does the ParakeetAI desktop app support?

The desktop app runs on Windows and macOS; in a browser, ParakeetAI needs desktop Chrome, which is also the way to use it on Linux. Other browsers aren't supported for live sessions, and the desktop app works whatever your default browser is.

### Which AI model does ParakeetAI use?

You can choose the AI model when you create or edit a session, from the model dropdown. Options include several Gemini, GPT, and Claude models, with a fast default selected for you. Lighter models respond faster; larger models can give more detailed answers. If your remembered model ever changes on its own, an app update either reset it to the current default or moved a retired model to its replacement (Claude 4.5 Sonnet now runs as Claude Sonnet 5, and GPT-5.6 Sol as GPT-5.6 Terra) — just pick your model again.

### Can I use ParakeetAI for meetings or online exams, not just interviews?

Yes. When you create a session you choose the mode — interview, or regular call for meetings and other conversations. It also works during online exams. Interview mode additionally uses your uploaded CV or resume; regular call mode doesn't, since it's built for conversations where that isn't relevant. Everything else — documents, instructions, transcripts, notes — works the same in both.
