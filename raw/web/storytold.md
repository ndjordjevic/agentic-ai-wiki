## ArtCraft flagship README excerpt — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/storytold/artcraft/main/README.md
ArtCraft is the IDE for interactive AI image and video creation. Compose in 2D, stage scenes in 3D, and choose the models that fit your work. Turn prompting into *crafting* with visual tools for precise, repeatable results.

Advanced crafting features (per the README's feature table): Image to Location (place virtual actors in a consistent environment across multiple shots), 3D Image Compositing (layer backdrops/foregrounds/props with depth), 2D Image Compositing (image layers, background removal, drawing tools), Image to 3D Mesh, Character Posing, Scene Blocking with Kitbashing (combine 3D asset kits), Character Identity Transfer (posed mannequin guides character placement/pose), Background Removal, and Mixed Asset Crafting (combine image cutouts, worlds, and 3D meshes).

Quick/easy prompting path: Text to Image (explore ideas across a range of image models from a text prompt) and Prompted Image Editing (describe changes to an image with models such as Nano Banana Pro and GPT Image).

Social: Discord (discord.gg/artcraft), YouTube (@OfficialArtCraftStudios), X (@get_artcraft), LinkedIn (artcraft-ai). Download at getartcraft.com; source/build instructions at `./_docs/dev_setup.md` in the repo.
