# Wispr Flow

> Voice-to-text dictation app that lets you type 4x faster by speaking naturally. Works anywhere you can type on macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android with AI-powered transcription in 100+ languages.

For comprehensive documentation, see [llms-full.txt](https://wisprflow.ai/llms-full.txt).

Wispr Flow transforms your voice into text in any application without requiring integrations. It uses advanced AI models for accurate, context-aware transcription that understands your writing style and adapts to it. Key capabilities include AI auto-edits that fix grammar and filler words, personal dictionary for names and jargon, voice snippets for reusable templates, and enterprise-grade security with SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, and ISO 27001 compliance.
