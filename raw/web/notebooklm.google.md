## Learn about NotebookLM — https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16164461

NotebookLM is an AI-powered research assistant designed to help refine and organize ideas. Capabilities:
- Upload PDFs, websites, YouTube videos, audio files, Google Docs, or Google Slides or discover new sources.
- Experience Gemini's advanced reasoning and interaction across text, graphs, images, audio, and 80+ languages.
- Chat with your notebook to get grounded information based on sources with clear in-line citations.
- Transform sources into approachable formats: study guides, briefings, audio overviews, mind maps, and more.

**Availability**: 80+ languages; supported in 150+ countries/territories. Requires a Google Account. Available on personal, work, and school accounts (Workspace admin must enable for work/school). Users under 18 have stricter content policies.

**Requirements**: Google Account (personal or work/school); must be signed in.

**Why NotebookLM may not answer**: safety flags on source content; unclear phrasing; information not in uploaded sources.

**Data protection**: Work/Workspace data governed by Google Workspace Terms of Service. Individual user data not used to train models unless feedback is shared. Only safety/reliability processing (fraud, abuse, technical issue prevention) may occur.

## Generate Audio Overview — https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16212820

Audio Overviews are deep-dive discussions between AI hosts providing in-depth summaries of key topics from uploaded sources. Designed as objective reflections of source content.

### Formats
- **Deep Dive (default)**: Two AI hosts unpack and connect topics in a lively conversation.
- **The Brief**: Single speaker delivers key takeaways in under two minutes.
- **The Critique**: Two hosts provide constructive evaluation of material (essays, design docs).
- **The Debate**: Two hosts engage in a formal back-and-forth debate on the topic.

### Customization
- Choose format, language (80+ languages), and length (Shorter / Default / Longer, English only).
- Input a prompt to focus on specific topics or adjust expertise level.

### Interactive mode (English only)
- Join a live conversation and ask the AI hosts questions by voice.
- Hosts respond with personalized answers based on sources, then resume the original overview.

### Sharing and download options
- Share via link (requires notebook shared with recipient or made public — consumer accounts only).
- Share the entire notebook for collaborators to access via Studio panel.
- Download audio file for external sharing.

### Notes
- Audio, including voices, is AI-generated and may contain inaccuracies or glitches.
- Supports parallel generation alongside other artifacts.
- Listening while working in NotebookLM is supported; citations and source queries available during playback.
