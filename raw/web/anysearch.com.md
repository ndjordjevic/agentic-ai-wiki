1. **Secure & Private by Design** — featuring "Zero Retention Execution" with in-path processing and zero-knowledge credentials
2. **Unified Integration** — providing "one unified entry point to access extensive, high-quality data sources"
3. **Agent-Native Design** — offering structured output and support for API, MCP, and Skill integrations
4. **Smart Intent Routing** — automatically identifying query intent and routing to relevant sources
5. **Full-Spectrum Coverage** — spanning multiple domains from professional to everyday needs
